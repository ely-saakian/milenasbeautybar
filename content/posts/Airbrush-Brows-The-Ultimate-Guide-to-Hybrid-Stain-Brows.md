## What Are Airbrush Brows?

Airbrush Brows are a modern brow technique that uses a professional airbrush device to gently mist pigment onto the brows and skin.

This creates a **smooth, even stain** that mimics the look of softly filled-in brows—without harsh lines or patchiness.

- Soft, ombré finish
- Custom color to fit your face
- Defined appearance

## What Is Hybrid Stain Dye?

Hybrid Stain Dye is a professional brow color that combines:

- The **rich intensity of henna**
- The **ease and flexibility of tint**

This allows for a more **long-lasting, defined result** while still looking soft and natural.

## Brow Prep & Aftercare (For Long-Lasting Results)

To get the most out of your Airbrush Brows, proper aftercare is essential:

**After your appointment:**

- Avoid makeup and cleansers around the brows for 24 hours
- Avoid sun exposure, saunas, steam, and pools for 24 hours
- Avoid exfoliating or anti-aging products near the brows for 72 hours
- Avoid self-tanner on the face for 2–3 days
- Do not touch or over-handle the brows

Following these steps helps extend both the **skin stain and hair color**

### Contraindications

- Recent operation (within 6 months for minor surgery, 2 years for major surgery)
- Cuts or abrasions
- Bruising, swelling, or inflammation
- Sunburn or windburn
- Chemotherapy (current cancer treatment)
- Scabies
- Dermatitis or psoriasis in the treatment area
- Viral or bacterial infections
- Pediculosis (lice)
- Alopecia
- Trichotillomania
- Eczema, dermatitis, or psoriasis affecting brow or lash hairs
- Recent chemical treatments in the area (within less than one week)
- Recent tattoo, microblading, or feather-touch brow work (must be fully healed and cleared by technician before treatment)
- Botox or dermal fillers (must have clearance from the administering provider)
- Previous reactions to brow treatments
- Use of certain anti-acne medications such as Roaccutane, doxycycline, Epiduo gel, or derivatives
- Use of anti-aging creams or medications such as Vitamin A, retinols, or Efudex
